Hello! I’m Sharmista (Mish), a building performance specialist dedicated to designing zero-carbon, high-performance buildings that support sustainable, livable communities and foster social and environmental well-being.

As a Graduate Enclosure Consultant at Walter P Moore in Dallas, I evaluate building performance through hygrothermal and thermal analysis, infrared thermography for mission-critical data centres, and envelope energy code compliance with ASHRAE 90.1 and IECC. My work spans the full building performance spectrum including whole-building energy modeling that has cut predicted energy use by 20–35%, Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) analysis of airflow, thermal comfort, and data center heat transfer, daylighting studies, and embodied carbon research.

I hold an M.S. in Architecture (High-Performance Buildings) from Georgia Tech and I’m driven to bring data-informed performance thinking into every stage of design. I’m always excited to connect with teams using building performance analysis and sustainable design to shape better buildings. If you have any questions or would like to reach out to me, please don’t hesitate to contact me at [sharmista.deb25@gmail.com](sharmista.deb25@gmail.com).
