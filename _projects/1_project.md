## Overview

This project focuses on designing an exploration for building renovation to understand the tradeoff between occupancy (density), comfort, and energy use intensity while balancing operational energy, reducing overall carbon emissions, and maintaining daylight performance (UDI/ASE). Additionally, explore rainwater harvesting potential.

## Key Objectives

- Understand tradeoffs between occupancy density, comfort, and energy use intensity
- Balance operational energy and reduce overall carbon emissions
- Maintain optimal daylight performance (UDI/ASE metrics)
- Explore rainwater harvesting potential for sustainable water management

## Methodology

The project employs comprehensive building performance simulation to:
- Analyze various renovation scenarios
- Optimize building envelope and systems
- Evaluate daylight distribution and thermal comfort
- Assess energy consumption patterns
